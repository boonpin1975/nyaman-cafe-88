OUR STORY & PASSION

Where Thai Culinary Art Meets Balik Pulau Hospitality

Nestled in the lush, serene town of Balik Pulau, Nyaman Cafe 88 was born out of a love for genuine, uncompromised Thai food. "Nyaman" reflects comfort, warmth, and pure satisfaction—a feeling we deliver with every dish.

Our master chefs source fresh galangal, kaffir lime, krapow basil, and chili directly to ensure each bowl of Tom Yum vibrates with authentic sour, spicy, and savory harmony.

Rich Homemade Pastes

Curry pastes pounded daily from scratch using fresh roots & chili.
