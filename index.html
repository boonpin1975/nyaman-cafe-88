OUR STORY & PASSION

Where Thai Culinary Art Meets Balik Pulau Hospitality

Nestled in the lush, serene countryside of Balik Pulau, Nyaman Cafe 88 was born out of a passion for genuine, uncompromised Thai food. "Nyaman" reflects warmth, comfort, and pure satisfaction—a feeling served in every dish.

Our master chefs source fresh galangal, kaffir lime, holy basil, and Thai chilies directly to ensure each bowl of Tom Yum vibrates with authentic sour, spicy, and savory harmony.
